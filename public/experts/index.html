What the database is

Not a list of names. A list of yeses.

Anyone can scrape a list of names. What we keep is your yes, on terms you agreed to, and your goals in your own words. That is why brands come to us, and why you only hear about things that fit.

Invite-only.

Most experts join because we asked them. You can also apply. Either way, we talk first, and nobody is added without saying yes.

For practitioners, not influencers.

We look for people whose influence comes from the work: the calls you made, the teams you ran, the things you have seen go right and wrong. Follower counts are not the point.

You approve your own profile.

Your profile says what you know and what you want from it. You check it, fix it and approve it. We match on your goals, not just your title.

Private by design.

We never publish the directory, so you never become a cold list for vendors. A client sees you only on a match, and only once you both say yes. Nobody is told who said no.

How it works

Five steps. On your schedule.

Step 01

We connect.

Usually we reach out on LinkedIn because something you wrote caught our eye. A short note, no ask. Found us first? Apply here and we'll take it from there.

01
02
Step 02

We talk.

A short call to hear what you know and what you want from it. No prep. If the timing is wrong, we pick a date to come back.

Step 03

You apply.

We send you your profile, filled in from our call. Check it, fix anything we got wrong, and approve it. That is your application. Found us first? You fill it in yourself.

03
04
Step 04

We welcome you.

A welcome email says you're in and what to expect. It links your one-sheet, so you see yourself the way a client will.

Step 05

You hear by email on a fit.

When a client's piece fits your goals and topics, we email you. Yes or no, do your best to reply within 5 business days. No fit for a while? You won't hear from us.

05
What we ask in return

Three things. That's the deal.

Do your best to reply to new opportunities within 5 business days, yes or no. Share firsthand experience from your work as a practitioner. When a piece goes live, repost it to your network with your own thoughts, at the time we schedule, always within your workday. The full expert participation terms are one click away.
