Expert community

Expert participation terms.

What you agree to when you tick the box on your expert profile. Plain words, no surprises. Last updated October 31, 2026.

1. The three things we ask

  • Response time. When we email you about a new opportunity, you reply within 5 business days. A yes or a no both count.
  • Repost. When a piece you appear in goes live, you share it to your network with your own thoughts, not a bare share, at the time we schedule. We always schedule it within your workday.
  • Participation. You share firsthand experience from your work as a practitioner: real decisions, real results, in your own words.

2. Permission to feature you

When you take part in a piece, you give Splendid Engines and the brand the piece is made with permission to record, edit, publish and promote it, in any format and channel, including:

  • your name, job title, company and the topics you speak to;
  • your face, voice and image, from video, audio or photos you take part in or send us;
  • your quotes and what you say in recordings;
  • your headshot and any logo or brand assets you give us.

This permission lasts for as long as the piece is published. We don't charge for it, and you don't owe us anything for it.

We will never present you as endorsing a product, or put words in your mouth. If we quote you, it's something you said.

3. You see it before anyone else

  • Review. You get to see each piece before it goes live, and to fix anything that's wrong about you or your work.
  • Your sign-off chain. If your manager, PR or legal team needs to approve, tell us on your profile. We build their review into the schedule.
  • Saying no. You can pass on any opportunity, at any time, for any reason. Nobody is told who said no.

4. Your ideas stay yours

You keep everything you know and every idea you bring. The finished piece belongs to us and the brand it's made with, and you can always share it, link to it and use it to show your work.

5. Your employer and confidential information

Share only what you're free to share. Don't tell us anything confidential to your employer or anyone else. If your employer has a policy on public speaking or content, you're responsible for following it. Tell us if it changes what you can do.

6. Unpaid unless we agree otherwise

Taking part is voluntary and unpaid, unless we agree something different with you in writing for a specific piece. Being in the community doesn't make you an employee, contractor or agent of Splendid Engines or of any brand.

7. Your information

  • What we keep. Your profile: name, email, LinkedIn, the details you give us on your profile, and the terms you agreed to.
  • How we use it. To match you with pieces that fit, and to contact you about them. We don't sell it, and we don't put you on a marketing list.
  • Who sees it. Our team, and a brand only when you've said yes to working with them. Brands never see the full community.
  • Your control. Ask us any time to see, change or delete your information. Reply to any email from us.

8. Leaving

You can leave the community at any time by replying to any email from us. We stop sending you opportunities and remove your profile. Pieces already published stay live, because the brand and other contributors are in them too. If you ask, we stop promoting them with your name from then on.

9. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we email you first. If you don't want to continue under the new terms, you can leave as described above.

Questions about any of this? Reply to any email from us, or get in touch.
