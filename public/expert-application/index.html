The checkboxes, in plain words

Three things we ask of you.

Each one is its own box on the form. Tick all three, plus the participation terms, and you're in.

01 · Response time

Reply within 5 business days.

When a client wants to co-create on your topic, we email you. A yes or a no within 5 business days keeps things moving. A no is always fine.

02 · Repost

Repost with your thoughts.

When a piece you're in goes live, share it to your network with a line or two of your own take, at the time we schedule. That's always within your workday. Your take is why people read it.

03 · Participation

Share what you've done.

Clients come to us for people who've done the work. We ask about real decisions and real results from your work as a practitioner, never a pitch.

The fourth box is the expert participation terms: permission to feature you, how review works, and how to leave.

A note from us

We do not spam. Ever.

Your info lives in our database and goes nowhere else. Once your profile is in, we send a welcome email. Then you hear from us by email when there is a real match and a real project. If nothing fits for a year, you will not hear from us. That is a feature, not a bug.
