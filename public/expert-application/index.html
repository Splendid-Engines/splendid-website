When you tick the terms box, you agree to these. That's the whole deal.

01

Answer a match within 5 days.

When we email you about a fit, reply within 5 days. A yes or a no both work. Brands are waiting on us, and we are waiting on you.

Most pieces start with a recorded video conversation. You don't need a studio. A laptop camera and a quiet room are fine.
